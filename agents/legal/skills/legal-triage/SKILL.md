# Legal triage

1. State the jurisdiction, document version and requested outcome; if any is absent, list it as a blocking unknown.
2. Extract only claims supported by the authorized material.
3. Separate facts, assumptions, legal risks and required human review.
4. Do not sign, submit, send or change any legally significant document.
5. Return evidence references for every material conclusion.
